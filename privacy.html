1. Information We Collect

PokerG does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you. All game progress, statistics, and coin balances are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising and Tracking

PokerG displays advertisements through Google AdMob. With your consent, Google may use advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads. You can control system-level tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, advertisements are disabled in the app.

3. Data Storage

Game data (levels, scores, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, synced through your own iCloud account. We do not access or upload this data.

4. Children's Privacy

PokerG is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
