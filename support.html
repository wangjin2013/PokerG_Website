Frequently Asked Questions

Is PokerG free?

Yes. PokerG is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" and coin packs) are available.

How do I remove ads?

Purchase the "Remove Ads" item from the Settings screen. Ads will be disabled after the purchase completes.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Your purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Where does game data come from?

All game content is built into the app. Game progress and coin balances are stored locally on your device.

Do you collect my personal data?

No. See our Privacy Policy for details.
